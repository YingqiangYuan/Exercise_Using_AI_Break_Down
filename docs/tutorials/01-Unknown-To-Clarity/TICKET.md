# Exercise: Using AI to Break Down Unfamiliar Information

## Objective

Apply the "Three Elements of Information Breakdown" method by choosing a real-world scenario — filing taxes or signing a lease — and working with AI to make sense of official documents and produce a practical reference guide.

By the end of this exercise, you'll have the confidence to face any unfamiliar, complex information without panicking — knowing what to ask and how to get answers.

**Prerequisite: Please read the tutorial《[Using AI to Break Down Unfamiliar Information: From "Completely Lost" to "I've Got This"](https://github.com/easyscalecloud/learn_claude_ai_problem_solving_general-project/tree/01-Unknown-To-Clarity/)》first.**

**Estimated time: 25–35 minutes**

---

## How-to Guide

### Step 1: Pick Your Scenario (2 minutes)

Choose whichever scenario is more relevant to your life right now:

| Option | Scenario | Best for |
|--------|----------|----------|
| **A** | Filing taxes for the first time — understanding your W-2 | Anyone currently working or about to start working |
| **B** | Renting your first apartment — understanding a lease agreement | Anyone currently renting or about to sign a lease |

### Step 2: Get the Official Documents (3 minutes)

**Option A (W-2 Tax Form):**

- W-2 form and employee instructions: https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/fw2.pdf
- Full instructions document (more detailed): https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-pdf/iw2w3.pdf

**Option B (Lease Agreement):**

- Open the official California landlord-tenant guide: https://www.dre.ca.gov/publications/ResourceGuidebook/2025_Landlord_Tenant_Guide.pdf

### Step 3: Prepare Your Questions (5 minutes)

Before you start talking to the AI, write down the following:

1. **Background:** Who are you? What's your situation?
2. **Goals:** What do you want the AI to help you accomplish? (List at least 2–3 goals.)
3. **Additional context:** Are there any personal circumstances or constraints the AI should know about?

**Important:** Include this line in your prompt to ask the AI to cite its sources:

> "When you answer my questions, please reference the relevant sections of the original document (such as page numbers or direct quotes) so I can cross-check your explanations."

### Step 4: Have a Multi-Turn Conversation with AI (15–20 minutes)

- Provide the official document to the AI (upload the file or share the link)
- Use the three elements you prepared to frame your opening question
- Follow up on the AI's responses — dig deeper into the details
- **Important:** At the end, ask the AI to generate an Artifact document as your final deliverable

### Step 5: Save and Submit (5 minutes)

- Save the Artifact document the AI generated
- Save your conversation with the AI (screenshots or a link to the chat)
- Send everything to your mentor
