# AI Problem-Solving Syllabus

This syllabus tracks the learning progression across all lessons in this course.

---

## 01-Unknown-To-Clarity

**From Confusion to Clarity: Using AI to Break Down Unfamiliar Information**

This lesson teaches you how to transform confusing official documents, emails, or web pages into actionable understanding. You'll learn the "Three Elements Framework" (Context, Goal, Additional Info) for effective AI questioning, practice with real-world scenarios like tax forms or rental agreements, and develop the critical habit of asking AI to cite sources for verification. After completing this lesson, you can confidently tackle any unfamiliar document by structuring your questions properly and validating AI responses against original sources.

---

## 02-Let-AI-Ask-You

**The Reverse Questioning Method: Let AI Ask You First**

Building on the Three Elements Framework from Lesson 01, this lesson addresses a common barrier: not knowing what information to provide. You'll master the "Reverse Questioning Method" — simply add "Ask me a few questions first, then answer" to your query. Practice controlling the conversation flow by telling AI to "keep asking" or "that's enough." After this lesson, you can get personalized AI responses even when you're unsure how to frame your question.
